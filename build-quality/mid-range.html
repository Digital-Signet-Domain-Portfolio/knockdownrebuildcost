Mid-range new build cost

Mid-range is Build It's average 2026 self build: £2,500 to £3,000/sqm, a main contractor building to a fairly typical self-build spec, better than Building Regulations but short of luxury. Homebuilding & Renovating's typical 200-250 sqm self build at £575,000 to £600,000 works out at about £2,300 to £3,000/sqm, close to the same band.

Oliver Wakefield-Smith · Updated 22 November 2026 · Reviewed against Build It build costs 2026 (10 July 2026), Homebuilding & Renovating build cost guide (16 February 2026)

Current £/sqm range

Build It: "In 2026, an average self build is likely to cost between £2,500-£3,000 per m²", and a main contractor building to a fairly typical self-build spec will "probably be looking at a cost of around £2,500-£3,000 per m²"[Build It 2026]. The figure covers the whole build, foundations and drainage included; demolition, fees and temporary accommodation sit on top in the calculator.

What a good finish costs

Homebuilding & Renovating's good specification: a kitchen with higher-quality units, quartz or granite worktops, branded appliances and an island £20,000 to £30,000; a main bathroom £11,000; a shower room £11,500; flooring around £85/sqm, with engineered oak at £110 to £140/sqm[Homebuilding 2026].

Cross-check: Homebuilding & Renovating

Homebuilding & Renovating's typical 200-250 sqm self build costs £575,000 to £600,000, about £2,300 to £3,000/sqm. Its build-route table puts a main contractor at £2,000 to £2,800/sqm and design and build at £2,300 to £3,500/sqm, excluding VAT[Homebuilding 2026].

Why most KDR owners land here

A replacement house is usually built by a main contractor for owners who will live in it, which is the profile behind Build It's average figure. Moving up to premium is mostly a fit-out decision: the kitchen alone runs from £20,000 to £30,000 at good spec to £35,000 to £75,000+ at excellent spec on Homebuilding & Renovating's figures.
