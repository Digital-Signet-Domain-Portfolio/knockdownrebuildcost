How the calculator builds the number

The build line is your floor area x the £/sqm for your tier x your region's adjustment. The tiers come from Build It's published 2026 figures: £2,200 for a cost-conscious build, £2,500 to £3,000 on average, over £3,200 for high-end and £5,000 to £6,000 with all the bells and whistles[Build It 2026]. The regional adjustment is Build It's table (South East 104%, Scotland 105%, Rest of UK 100%), with the BCIS location index for Wandsworth (126) standing in for London. Build It's £/sqm includes foundations and drainage, so they are not added again.

Around the build sit seven more lines: demolition £10,000 to £15,000, the £610 planning fee for one new dwelling in England, Building Regulations fees of £1,000 to £3,500, design fees of 10-15% of the build, insurance and warranty at 1-2%, contingency at 10-15%, and your months of rent[Homebuilding 2026].

What is excluded

Land, asbestos surveys and removal, CIL (a self build can claim exemption before work starts), party wall costs, landscaping, pools, basements and detached garages. Demolition is Build It's and Homebuilding & Renovating's budget for an old house; contamination or difficult access costs more.

Looking for an insurance rebuild value?

A buildings-insurance rebuild value is a different number: what it would cost to rebuild your existing house as it stands. The ABI and BCIS publish a free calculator for that; see insurance rebuild cost vs knockdown rebuild cost.

What it is not

This is not a quote and it does not bind a contractor. It is a defensible reference range to take into a Stage 2 conversation with your architect and into a self-build mortgage application. If your final tender comes in significantly outside the range, ask why; if it comes in inside, the calculator did its job.
