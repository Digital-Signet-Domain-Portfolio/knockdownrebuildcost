Sources ledger

Every numeric claim on this site traces to a primary public source. The ledger lists what each figure is, what value we hold, where it comes from, when we last verified it, and when it is due for the next review. Treat this page as the trust artefact, not a methodology essay.

Each row corresponds to a citation chip elsewhere on the site. Click the citation chip on any numeric claim and you arrive at the relevant row here. The URL takes you to the underlying source. If a figure is past its next-review date, treat the on-site figure as provisional.

Sources we do not use

We do not infer specific £/sqm rates for vendors who do not publish them. We do not use AI-generated cost benchmarks. We do not quote contractor day-rates as if they were a published index. Where the source itself is uncertain (a forecast, a survey with a small sample), we note the uncertainty on the page rather than print a false-precision figure.

Corrections

If you spot an error or a figure past its next-review date, contact us via the about page. We update the on-site figure and increment the verified date once the correction is checked against the source.
