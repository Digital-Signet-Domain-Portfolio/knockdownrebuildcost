Sources we do not use

We do not infer specific £/sqm rates for vendors who do not publish them. We do not use AI-generated cost benchmarks. We do not quote contractor day-rates as if they were a published index. Where the source itself is uncertain (a forecast, a survey with a small sample), we note the uncertainty on the page rather than print a false-precision figure.

Corrections

If you spot an error or a figure past its next-review date, contact us via the about page. We update the on-site figure and increment the verified date once the correction is checked against the source.
