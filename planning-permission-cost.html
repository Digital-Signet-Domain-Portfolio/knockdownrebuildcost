Knockdown rebuild planning permission cost

A replacement dwelling is a full application for the erection of a new dwellinghouse: £610 in England at 2026 statutory rates (from 1 April 2026). It rises to £752 for applications made from 8 December 2026. Add the council's fee for pre-application advice, £283 to £596.40 for one dwelling at the lowest service level of the five councils we read (worth it on any constrained plot), plus a Section 80 demolition notice.

Statutory application fees

The Town and Country Planning (Fees for Applications) Regulations set fees nationally. A replacement dwelling is a full application for the erection of one new dwellinghouse, which costs £610 in England from 1 April 2026; a householder extension application, by contrast, is £548[Planning Portal]. Fees were uprated on 1 April 2026 (the new-dwelling application rose from £578 to £610). SI 2026/1192 raises it to £752 per dwellinghouse for applications made from 8 December 2026, and the government says fees will then rise with indexation on 1 April 2027[SI 2026/1192].

Each council sets its own pre-application charges; these are from their published fee pages, read 24 November 2026. Always worth it for replacement dwellings on Green Belt, AONB, or conservation-area plots; a pre-app letter that signals support before you spend on architect Stage 2 is the single best risk-reducer in the project.

Section 80 demolition notice

Building Act 1984 section 80 requires written notice to the local authority before demolition starts[Building Act 1984]. No fee; the authority has 6 weeks to respond with a Section 81 counter-notice setting site conditions (party-wall protection, dust, hours).

Determination period and common refusals

8 weeks statutory for householder, 13 weeks for major. In practice 12-20 weeks for replacement dwellings once consultee responses are factored in. Common refusal grounds: out-of-scale design (NPPF Para 84 test), Green Belt openness, AONB landscape impact, biodiversity net gain non-compliance, highways access. A good pre-app catches all five.
