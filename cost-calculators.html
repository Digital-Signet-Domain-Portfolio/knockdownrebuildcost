UK knockdown-rebuild cost calculators & tools

Ten independent UK references homeowners use to size a knockdown rebuild before paying RIBA Stage 2 fees. Each one is reviewed individually: what it covers, what it costs, what it gets right, where it falls short.

Oliver Wakefield-Smith · Updated 21 June 2026 · Reviewed against BCIS Q2 2026 TPI, Build It / Self Build & Design, Urbanist Architecture, Homebuilding & Renovating (Future plc), FMB, RIBA, Planning Portal, HMRC VAT Notice 708, LABC, Buildstore

The ten UK references at a glance

UK builders quote bespoke and only release their rates inside a tender pack. The references below are the publicly verifiable sources homeowners, architects, and quantity surveyors actually triangulate against. The calculator on this site is built on the FMB and Homebuilding & Renovating editorial benchmarks and HMRC VAT rules, with the BCIS All-in Tender Price Index used as a cost-movement check (it publishes index movement, not absolute £/sqm rates); this page exists so you can review each reference in isolation and see why the headline numbers vary.

Reference
BCIS (Building Cost Information Service)
Index access £660+/yr; free consumer rebuild calculator at abi.bcis.co.uk
Verified 2026-08-23

RIBA (Royal Institute of British Architects)
Typical one-off house fees 7-15% of build cost (Plan of Work Stages 1-6); lower for design-only

Planning Portal
£610 for a full application for the erection of one new dwellinghouse (a replacement dwelling, England, from 1 April 2026); £548 for a householder extension

HMRC VAT Notice 708
Zero-rated services from VAT-registered contractor; reclaim 20% on materials via DIY route within 6 months of completion
