Rebuild cost per square metre (UK)

A UK self build averages £2,500 to £3,000 per sqm in 2026 on Build It's figures (published 10 July 2026). The four tiers below are set from Build It's own published points, with Homebuilding & Renovating's build-route figures and BCIS estate-housing costs as cross-checks, and a regional adjustment for where you build.

Build It's figures are per square metre of completed floor space (gross internal area) and cover the whole build, foundations and drainage included: its cost breakdown puts foundations and drainage at 9% of the build[Build It 2026]. Demolition, design fees, planning, Building Regulations, insurance, contingency and temporary accommodation are added by the calculator.

Homebuilding & Renovating's average construction cost by build route[Homebuilding 2026]. Its typical 200-250 sqm self build at £575,000 to £600,000 works out at about £2,300 to £3,000/sqm, in line with Build It's average. A new replacement dwelling is zero-rated for VAT, so the ex-VAT figures compare directly.

BCIS sells its build-cost data, but councils reprint it in the viability studies behind their local plans. "Estate housing generally" is the cost of houses built in volume, including preliminaries but excluding external works, fees and contingency, so it sits well below a one-off self build. It is the nearest published figure for what a developer-spec house costs a housebuilder, and the location index shows how far each area sits from the UK average.

What is included and excluded

The £/sqm figures cover the house itself: substructure, superstructure, envelope, services and fit-out to the tier's spec. Excluded: land, demolition, professional fees, planning fees, CIL, party wall costs, Building Regulations fees, temporary accommodation, landscaping, pools, basements and detached garages. Homebuilding & Renovating says a basement costs 50-100% more per square metre than above-ground space. Always ask what a quoted £/sqm rate includes before comparing it with these figures.
