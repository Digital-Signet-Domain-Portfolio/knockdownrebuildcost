What is included and excluded

The £/sqm headline is build above slab: superstructure, envelope, services, fit-out to tier spec. Excluded: land, foundations, professional fees, planning fees, CIL, party-wall, BC, temporary accommodation, soft landscaping, pools, basements, detached garages. Always read the small print on a quoted £/sqm rate; the FMB cost guide [FMB 2026] defines the same boundary.
