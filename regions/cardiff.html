Local detail

Welsh planning policy is governed by Future Wales: the National Plan 2040, supplemented by Planning Policy Wales (PPW), and local Local Development Plans. Cardiff and the Vale of Glamorgan have separate LDPs. Replacement-dwelling policy follows PPW and is broadly aligned with NPPF principles.

Land Transaction Tax (LTT) replaces SDLT on plot purchases in Wales; the higher residential rate plus 4% additional-property surcharge applies on second-home purchases. Welsh Building Regulations now diverge from English (Part L 2022 Wales currently in force).

VAT zero-rating under HMRC Notice 708 applies UK-wide and is unaffected by the Welsh planning regime. Self-build CIL exemption does not apply in Wales as Wales does not operate CIL; Section 106 obligations may apply on larger schemes.
