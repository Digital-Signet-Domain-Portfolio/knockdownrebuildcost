Self build vs knockdown rebuild

Both routes deliver a brand-new dwelling at zero VAT under HMRC Notice 708. The cost gap is almost entirely upstream of construction: plot acquisition, plot premium, services connections, and planning risk. On comparable South East plots, KDR comes in £80k-£180k cheaper because the services, access, and consent are already proven.

Oliver Wakefield-Smith · Updated 21 June 2026 · Reviewed against HMRC VAT Notice 708, FMB / Homebuilding & Renovating editorial £/sqm benchmarks, Self Build Portal (NaCSBA), Buildstore self-build mortgage data

Build cost: roughly the same

The cost of building above slab is essentially identical. Both are new dwellings; both use the same model £/sqm bands. Mid-tier South East: £2,000 to £2,500/sqm x 1.20 regional multiplier [Homebuilding 2026]. A 200 sqm new house lands at £480k to £600k above slab whether you bought a bare plot or are replacing a 1960s bungalow.

Where the routes diverge is what sits underneath that figure and what sits to the side of it.

The plot delta

The single biggest line in self-build is plot acquisition. UK serviced building plots in the South East run £180k to £450k for a single-house plot with planning, services to the boundary, and access agreed [market 2026]. On a KDR, you already own the plot. The accounting equivalent is the value of the existing house on the day you swing the wrecking ball, which is sunk cost.

Plot premiums in NaCSBA-tracked custom-build areas can be 20-40% above raw-residential land per square metre because of the consent + services premium.

VAT: zero on both

A self-build new dwelling and a KDR replacement dwelling are both zero-rated for VAT under HMRC VAT Notice 708 section 3 [HMRC 708]. Self-builders use the DIY route (VAT431NB, reclaim within 6 months of completion). KDR can use either the DIY route or have the contractor invoice at 0% with a customer certificate. There is no VAT advantage either way.

0% VAT. New dwelling (VAT Notice 708)

Planning risk

Self-build on a new plot starts with planning consent or it doesn't happen, the plot value is roughly half without it. KDR usually has a high probability of consent because there is already a dwelling on the plot; replacement-dwelling policy in most LPAs allows like-for-like or modest uplift. Conservation areas, AONB, and listed buildings break both routes equally. Plot-finder-supplied plots usually come with consent already in place, eliminating this risk at the cost of paying the premium up-front.

Services and access

On a KDR plot you inherit the existing water, gas, electric, drainage, and BT connections, typically £0 to £3k to keep live during demolition and adapt. On a virgin plot, services connections run £8k to £25k: water £3k-£8k, electric £5k-£12k, gas £2k-£5k, BT/openreach £0-£2k, foul drainage £2k-£10k depending on distance to sewer. Add an access dropped-kerb at £700-£2,000. This is real money that self-builders almost always under-budget.

Finance: different products

Self-build mortgages (Buildstore, BuildLoan, Hanley Economic, Newbury, Ecology, etc.) work the same way for both, stage payments either in arrears (LIBOR-era lenders) or in advance (BuildStore Accelerator). KDR has one extra option: a bridging facility against the existing house equity for ~12 months until the new dwelling is mortgaged. Self-build can't do that because there's no existing house to bridge against; you need the plot purchase under a separate plot-only mortgage product first.

Timeline

Self-build adds plot search and purchase (3-12 months) and services connections (8-16 weeks) on top of the standard 18-24 month build. KDR is faster end-to-end because both of those are already done. KDR is also faster to start because you skip the plot-finder funnel.

All-in headline on a 200 sqm South East mid-tier project

Self build: £180k-£450k plot + £640k-£870k all-in build (matches our calculator output for the build half) = £820k to £1.32M total, less if you find an unserviced plot and absorb services into the build budget.

Knockdown rebuild: £640k to £870k all-in (the calculator on the homepage returns this for the same inputs), with the existing house value as sunk cost. £640k to £870k incremental.

On the same plot, KDR is a smaller cheque to write. On comparable end-houses, the question is whether you want to keep your existing location or move to a plot with the topography, view, and orientation you actually want.
